# 守灯人 The Lantern Keeper

A Ghost Festival (中元节) match-3 roguelike. On the night the Ghost Gate opens, Grandma's lantern is found snuffed out. Bao the panda descends through the underworld, across 12 lanterns and three acts, to bring her soul home before dawn.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Everything lives in that one file; the only external request is Google Fonts.

On iPhone: open the GitHub Pages link in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and the game launches full-screen like an app.

## Features in this version

- **English / 中文:** tap the 中 / EN button at the top to switch the whole game, story included. The choice is remembered.
- **Beginner tutorial:** new players get a short guided fight. It can be replayed from the start screen.
- **Second chance:** once per fight, take a 15-second break with a cat or dog clip or a music video (The Weeknd, BTS, NewJeans) to revive with half HP. In trials it gives 5 extra moves. Free, no ads: the clips are official YouTube embeds.

## How it plays

- **Match tiles to act.** Firecrackers attack, jade shields, peaches heal, joss gold gives coins and chi, bells exorcise enemy marks.
- **Special tiles.** 2×2 makes a rocket, 4 in a row a line blast, an L or T a lantern bomb, 5 in a row a lucky ball. Swap two specials together for a combo.
- **Answer the enemy.** Enemies mark tiles with ghost fire, drool or curses. Clear the marked tiles before the countdown ends to cancel that part of the attack. Hit a charging enemy hard enough to interrupt it.
- **Roguelike run.** Pick your road at each lantern: battles, trials with goals (frost, lost souls, tombstones, seals), the Ghost Market, the Ancestral Shrine, strange encounters. Level up, collect relics, face three guardians.
- **Losing isn't the end.** Each run has lantern flames that restart the current act. Every run earns incense for lasting blessings in the Ancestral Hall. Progress auto-saves at each lantern.
